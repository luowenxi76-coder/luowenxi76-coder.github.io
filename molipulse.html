[S03] lexmount/moli 历史案例回放

consoleAPICalled events arrive after evaluate response under awaitPromise

作者 / 维护者原话短摘（@XDLCS）
“With Runtime.enable active and a long-running Runtime.evaluate (awaitPromise: true), some Runtime.consoleAPICalled events produced during evaluation arrive after the evaluate response instead of before it. Agents that treat the evaluate response as completion and then close miss the tail of console output.”
运营判断（可核验推演）
Puppeteer/Playwright 生态默认把 Runtime.evaluate 返回当作脚本日志已刷新的信号；社区贡献者在 Discussion #3465 中提到已针对该问题提交 PR #3471，需跟进研发合入进度并校验 CDP 时序兼容性。
